# Telco Customer Churn Analysis

Business analytics project completed for ABC Communications Ltd (internship assessment),
analyzing the Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers) to identify churn drivers
and recommend retention strategies.

## Key Findings
- Overall churn rate: 26.5%
- Month-to-month contracts churn at 42.7% vs 2.8% for two-year contracts (~15x)
- Customers in their first 12 months churn at 48.3%
- Fiber optic customers churn at 41.9% despite being the premium product
- Electronic check users churn at 45.3%, ~3x automatic-payment customers
